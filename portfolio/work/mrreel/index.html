OVERVIEW

Turning complex content into a simple creation experience.

Mr’Reel is an Arabic-first AI product created around two main use cases: transforming long-form educational content into short visual Reels, and helping businesses generate marketing videos using strong hooks and brand assets.

The design challenge was not only visual. The product had to simplify a technically complex AI workflow while still giving users enough control over content, style, voice, characters, and branding.

01Arabic-first

RTL interface designed from the beginning, not mirrored later.

02Multi-format

Supports PDF, Word, PowerPoint, images, videos, and text.

03Two key tracks

Learning transformation and marketing content generation.

04Guided creation

A step-by-step flow that keeps AI complexity in the background.

THE CHALLENGE

AI can be powerful — but the interface should not feel complicated.

01

Long-form content is hard to consume

Users may have valuable content, but reviewing or repurposing large documents takes time and effort.

02

Video creation has too many steps

Scriptwriting, editing, voice-over, motion, branding, and export can overwhelm non-technical users.

03

AI often removes user control

Automation is useful, but users still need to review, refine, and decide before the final output is generated.

Progressive Disclosure

Only show the settings users need at each stage instead of exposing the full complexity at once.

One Primary Action

Each step has one visually dominant next action so the creation flow always feels clear.

User Control

AI makes suggestions, but users can edit topics, change styles, and refine the output before generation.

OUTCOME & LEARNINGS

The strongest solution was not adding more AI — it was reducing visible complexity.

This project reinforced the importance of progressive disclosure in AI products. Users do not need to understand every technical step behind generation; they need to know where they are, what they can change, and what will happen next.

Because Mr’Reel is still presented here as a product design case study, I avoid inventing performance metrics. The value of this case study is in the product structure, workflow decisions, RTL thinking, and the reusable design system created around the experience.
