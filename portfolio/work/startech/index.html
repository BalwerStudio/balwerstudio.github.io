OVERVIEW

Making network access feel familiar, clear, and low-friction.

STARTECH is a hotspot internet service built around prepaid access cards and service points. The product needed more than a login page; it required a complete customer-facing experience that guided users before, during, and after connection.

The design therefore expanded into a small service ecosystem covering login, status, package discovery, support, point-of-sale information, recovery, maintenance, and logout states.

0112+ service states

From login to success, errors, maintenance, and logout.

02Card-based access

Users connect using a card number and password.

03Responsive UX

Designed for mobile-first access and larger service screens.

04Low technical barrier

Clear language and visible status for non-technical users.

THE CHALLENGE

Hotspot systems often expose technical states instead of user-friendly guidance.

01

Unclear login errors

Users need understandable feedback when credentials are invalid or service is unavailable.

02

Too much technical language

Connection, quota, and session states must be translated into clear service messages.

03

Scattered service information

Packages, support, and points of sale should be accessible without forcing users through extra steps.

OUTCOME & LEARNINGS

Service design improves when system states are translated into clear user decisions.

STARTECH helped me focus on a different type of UX problem than a typical app or marketing website: communicating service status clearly while keeping common actions immediately accessible.

The strongest design decisions came from reducing technical terminology, prioritizing connection status, and ensuring every error or interruption includes a clear next step for the user.
