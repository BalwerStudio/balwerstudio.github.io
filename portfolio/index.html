ABOUT ME

I design with clarity, intention, and the user in mind.

I’m Ahmad Yaser Sammour, a UI/UX Designer focused on creating digital experiences that feel clear, useful, and visually balanced.

My approach starts with understanding the problem before designing the interface. I break complex ideas into simpler user flows, then turn those flows into thoughtful, usable, and consistent products.

With a background in multimedia technology and web development, I also understand the technical side of digital products, which helps me design with implementation in mind.

“

Good design should make the next step feel obvious.

Strong interfaces help users understand where they are, what they can do, and what happens next — without unnecessary friction.

HOW I WORK

From understanding the problem to delivering a usable product.

Every project is different, but my process follows a clear principle: understand first, design with purpose, and refine through feedback.

01

Understand

Goals, users, constraints, requirements, and the problem behind the screen.

02

Structure

User flows, information architecture, feature priority, and content hierarchy.

03

Explore

Wireframes and early concepts to test structure before visual polish.

04

Design

High-fidelity interfaces, components, responsiveness, and visual systems.

05

Validate

Prototypes, feedback, usability checks, and iterative improvements.

06

Deliver

Organized files, reusable components, specs, assets, and developer handoff.
